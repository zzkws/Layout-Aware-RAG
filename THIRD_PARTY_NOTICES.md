# Third-party models and software

Model weights are downloaded from their official sources and are not committed to this repository.

| Component | Purpose | Upstream | Declared license |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Harrier OSS v1 0.6B | Multilingual dense embeddings | https://huggingface.co/microsoft/harrier-oss-v1-0.6b | MIT |
| DocLayout-YOLO DocStructBench | Page element detection | https://huggingface.co/juliozhao/DocLayout-YOLO-DocStructBench | Apache-2.0 |
| BGE-small-en-v1.5 | Optional lightweight embeddings | https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 | MIT |

Provider-hosted models are optional and are configured by environment variable. Their usage remains subject to the provider's current terms.
